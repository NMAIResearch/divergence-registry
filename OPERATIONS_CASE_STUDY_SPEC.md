# Research operations case study specification

**Purpose.** This working specification defines a compact career-facing case study based on the
Divergence Registry publication programme. It is not a research finding and is not part of a
research deposit.

## Entry condition

Draft the case study only after the first C11 to C13 checkpoint has closed. Every quantity in the
case study comes from the completed wave report or the committed project history. Until then,
throughput, admission rate, failure rate and publication yield are unverified.

## Audience and length

The audience is research operations, programme operations and AI governance hiring teams. The
finished case study should be one compact web page or a maximum two-page PDF. An extra page is
permitted where a required limitation or verification note needs it.

## Required structure

### Operating problem

Versioned public records can support reproducible empirical work, but current-state pages and
unverifiable source routes cannot support historical comparisons. The programme therefore admits a
subject through source feasibility before research decisions begin.

### Operating model

Show the sequence:

`candidate register -> feasibility probe -> runner -> scope review -> author approval -> data and digest -> semantic review -> bundle decision -> publication gates`

Name the owner, input, acceptance condition and stop condition for each stage. Keep the research
judgement manual and automate schema, custody, digest and completeness checks.

### Controls

Explain these controls in plain language:

- register-first candidate selection;
- distinct runner and reviewer runs;
- source-byte custody and hashes;
- scope approval bound to the closed question and scope records;
- atomic claim and source traceability;
- negative-result retention;
- separate mechanical and semantic review;
- explicit stop before remote publication.

### Measured results

Report only values present in the checkpoint report. For each ratio, state numerator, denominator
and N. Suitable measures are:

- candidates probed and admitted;
- subjects reaching each stage;
- sources retrieved and authenticated;
- gate failures by class;
- subjects passing semantic review;
- findings selected for a public bundle;
- elapsed time by stage, if the run records it consistently.

Do not turn a clean gate result into a claim that the research conclusion is correct.

### Decision and adjustment

State whether register-first selection reduced unauthentic source intake in the checkpoint. Compare
against a defined baseline only if the baseline population and measurement are recorded. Otherwise
state that no comparable baseline was run.

Record which candidate rules were retained, revised or withdrawn after the checkpoint.

### Evidence

Link to the public bundle, reproducer and project repository after they exist. Maintain a separate
claim trace for every statement about project history, counts, dates or outcomes.

## Exclusions

Exclude internal reasoning, agent transcripts, model praise, workflow narration and unpublished
source material. Do not present planned candidates as completed work. Do not place this case study
inside a research deposit.

## Conflict disclosure

Where a completed subject includes OpenAI or its direct competitors, the research output carries
the applicable conflict disclosure. The case study states that AI systems assisted the controlled
workflow and that substantive support decisions required separate review. The final wording must
match the runs that actually occurred.
